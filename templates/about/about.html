Technical Framework

OpticMetric AI utilizes a multi-stage deep learning pipeline for automated glaucoma screening, designed to assist ophthalmologists with high-precision segmentation and robust diagnostic classification.

database

1. Dataset (REFUGE)

Utilizes the REFUGE Dataset (Retinal Fundus Glaucoma Challenge), providing a standardized cohort of gold-standard annotations for training segmentation and Logistic Regression classification tasks.

IMAGES 1200+ Scans
grid_view

2. ResNet34 U-Net Segmentation

Dual deep ResNet34 U-Net architectures process 512×512 fundus scans. Disc model v3 isolates optic disc contours, and Cup model v4 maps optic cup perimeters for 11-feature extraction.

MODELS Disc v3 / Cup v4
monitoring

3. 5-Classifier System

Evaluates the extracted 11-feature vector across 5 trained classifiers: Logistic Regression, SVM, XGBoost, MLP, and Extra Trees, with 4 user-selectable prediction filter modes.

ALGORITHMS 5 Models / 4 Filters
description

4. Clinical Output

Generates hospital-compliant diagnostic sheets. Outputs exact area and VCDR values alongside overlays to enable review and printing of ophthalmic patient records.

EXPORT Print & PDF
