menu_book Clinical Benchmark Analysis & Evaluation

The results show that the selected algorithms provide different performance characteristics rather than a single model being superior across every metric. SVM and MLP achieved the highest accuracy of 92.25%, while SVM provided the highest specificity. Logistic Regression achieved the highest recall and ROC-AUC, making it particularly effective at identifying glaucoma cases and distinguishing between the two classes. MLP achieved the highest F1-Score, while Extra Trees also provided a strong balance between precision, recall, and F1-Score.

The difference between the models highlights the importance of considering multiple evaluation metrics in glaucoma detection. Accuracy alone may not adequately represent the ability of a model to detect glaucoma cases, particularly when the number of positive cases is relatively smaller. Therefore, recall, specificity, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC were also considered when comparing the classifiers.

The segmentation stage also produced strong results. The U-Net with ResNet-34 encoder achieved a Dice score of 0.9513 for optic disc segmentation and 0.8734 for optic cup segmentation on the held-out segmentation validation set. The corresponding IoU values were 0.9080 for the disc and 0.7806 for the cup, demonstrating effective segmentation of the optic nerve head structures used for feature extraction.
